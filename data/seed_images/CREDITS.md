# Seed image credits

Demo images are from the Hugging Face dataset **zcs15/ImageNet-D**
(https://huggingface.co/datasets/zcs15/ImageNet-D), license **MIT**.

Images are from the dataset's `background` variation and were resized to a maximum of 800px for this demo. Used under the MIT license.

Note: the dataset Porcupine0476/University_CommonLostItems (also MIT) was evaluated first but ships as a single opaque archive that cannot be streamed per-image, so this MIT-licensed dataset was used instead.

Categories used: backpack, laptop, mug, sunglasses, umbrella, water_bottle.
